## WhiteoutFlakes

`src/diablo3-app/appearance.ts` and `src/diablo3-app/scene.ts` take format facts from WhiteoutFlakes
(`src/io/d3/d3_types.h`, `src/io/d3/d3_sno_cache.h`, `src/io/d3/d3_model_adapter.cpp`,
`src/renderer/profiles/diablo3/d3_surface_table.h`): the names of the engine's texture types, the ShaderMap
tag chain of the opaque pass, the engine's blend factor numbers and a clip's frames per tick. The RenderParams
field offsets were measured on the installed game. No WhiteoutFlakes code is included. WhiteoutFlakes is
distributed under the same BSD 3-Clause License as WhiteoutLib above (Copyright (c) 2026, Fernando Sahmkow).
